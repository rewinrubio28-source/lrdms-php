# Documented LGU records position references

These are documented titles from official LGU publications, not a verified current Manila plantilla. Historical reports establish documented use at publication, not current vacancies or staffing. Use the employee's appointment title when assigning a real account. Reference titles do not assign permissions or create user accounts.

The seed adds 18 titles, alongside the two Manila Council titles already seeded. Generic administrative titles and Records Officer specializations are retained as published; they are not necessarily separate job families. Some are supervisory or legislative support positions rather than dedicated Records Officer positions.
